Chicken Curry
  [1] Heat in a deep pan: 2 1/2 tbsp vegetable oil
  [2] Temper until fragrant: #1, 1 bay leaf, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cloves, 3 green cardamom pods
  [3] Saute 7-8 min until deep golden: #2, 3 onions finely chopped, 2 green chilies slit
  [4] Saute 1 min until aromatic: #3, 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  [5] Cook until soft and mushy: #4, 2 tomatoes pureed, 1/4 tsp turmeric, 3/4 tsp salt
  [6] Stir in on low heat until aromatic: #5, 1/4 cup yogurt, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp coriander powder
  [7] Fry 3 min until pale: #6, 1/2 kg chicken thigh, 1 tbsp cilantro
  [8] Cover and cook 4 min on low: #7
  [9] Simmer covered until the gravy thickens: #8, 1/2 cup hot water
  [10] Garnish and rest off the heat: #9, 1 tbsp cilantro
